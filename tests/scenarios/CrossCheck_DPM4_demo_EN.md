# CrossCheck DPM4 Demo Script

Minsu, Soyeon, and Taeho have until tonight to propose an app that would improve campus life. The demo follows **Taeho’s screen** throughout. The group chat is on the left. Taeho’s private chat with his Personal Agent is on the right.

## 1. Develop an idea in private and share it with the team

| Step | Screen | Dialogue and actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minsu · Group chat | “We need an idea for tonight’s proposal. What about putting all the campus cafeteria menus in one place? I always have to check each one separately.” |
| 2 | Group chat | **Taeho** “I’d use that. I usually check the menus right before class ends.” **Minsu** “We could put the prices next to the menus, too.” |
| 3 | Taeho · Private chat | **Taeho** “Menus and prices are useful, but sometimes I like both options. What else would help me pick?” **Personal Agent** “Think about the last time that happened. Did you go for the shorter walk, the place your friends were going, or the shorter line?” |
| 4 | Soyeon · Group chat | While Taeho thinks it over, Soyeon posts in the group chat. “Yeah, if both lunches are around the same price, I’d still be standing outside class trying to pick one.” |
| 5 | Taeho · Private chat | **Taeho** “Oh, I’d use that last one. Yesterday the line was out the door at noon, but at 12:30 I walked right in. Could we show when each cafeteria usually gets busy?” **Personal Agent** “Yeah. You could put the usual wait next to each menu. Then if both lunches look good, you’d know which way to walk.” Taeho keeps the idea in his private chat for now and continues following the group discussion. |
| 6 | Minsu · Group chat | “We’ve got menus and prices. What else could we show students to help them decide where to eat?” |
| 6a | Moderator → Personal Agents · Background | The Moderator quotes Minsu’s question in Team conversation and posts “I passed this question to everyone’s Personal Agent.” Each agent checks its user’s private ideas. Any suggestions appear only in that person’s private chat. |
| 7 | Taeho · Private chat → Group chat | A suggestion appears in Taeho’s private chat. **Personal Agent** “You just mentioned choosing the shorter line. Want to share that?” Taeho reviews the draft and posts it **under his own name**. “What about putting wait times next to the menus? If both places have something I like, I’d go for the shorter line.” |

## 2. Agree on what “wait times” means in the proposal

| Step | Screen | Dialogue and actions |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Minsu · Group chat | “Okay, I think we’ve got our idea. Can we settle on what we’re putting in tonight’s proposal?” |
| 9 | Moderator · Proposal confirmation card | The card reads, **“Students can compare campus cafeteria menus, prices, and wait times to decide where to eat.”** The Moderator asks, “Does this capture what you want to propose?” Minsu and Soyeon confirm. Taeho pauses at the words *wait times*. |
| 9a | Taeho · Private chat | **Taeho** “Does ‘wait times’ sound like we’d show how long the line is right now?” **Personal Agent** “It could. You mentioned showing when each cafeteria usually gets busy. Want to ask which one the team means?” |
| 9b | Taeho · Group chat | Taeho asks **under his own name**, “Quick check before we submit. Are we promising live wait times, or the usual wait at that time of day?” |
| 10 | Group chat | **Minsu** “Oh, I was picturing a live estimate.” **Taeho** “I meant the usual wait. We could collect that ourselves, but I’m not sure how we’d keep a live estimate updated.” **Soyeon** “The usual wait would be enough for me. I’d just want to know if noon is a bad time to go.” **Minsu** “Okay, let’s go with that.” |
| 11 | Moderator · Revised proposal card | “Students can compare campus cafeteria menus, prices, and **typical wait times by day and time** to decide where to eat.” The card highlights the revised phrase. All three read the updated proposal and select **Confirm**. |

## 3. Use private replies to settle on a meeting time

**Role notes for rehearsal.** The main gate works for everyone. Each teammate has a different schedule on Tuesday afternoon.

| Teammate | What to keep in mind |
|---|---|
| Minsu | Your afternoon is flexible. |
| Soyeon | You can meet at 5:00 PM, but you need to leave by 5:30 PM. |
| Taeho | You’ll be coming from Daejeon Station, so 5:30 PM is easier. You can make it by 5:00 PM if you leave a little earlier. You want to suggest the later time anonymously and keep your starting point private. |

| Step | Screen | Dialogue and actions |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Minsu · Group chat | “I’ll submit the proposal tonight and sketch the comparison screen before we meet. How about Tuesday afternoon somewhere near campus? Half an hour should be enough to go over it.” |
| 13 | Moderator → Personal Agents · Private chats | Through each teammate’s Personal Agent, the Moderator asks, **“What time on Tuesday afternoon works for you, and where would be easiest to meet?”** Minsu replies that he’s flexible. Soyeon replies that she’s free from 5:00 to 5:30. Keep the demo on Taeho’s private chat. |
| 13a | Taeho · Private reply | **Taeho** “I’ll be coming from Daejeon Station, so 5:30 by the main gate would be easiest. Can you suggest that without my name?” His Personal Agent previews the wording it will pass to the Moderator. **“Prefer 5:30 PM at the main gate.”** Taeho approves. His starting point stays in his private chat. |
| 14 | Taeho · Private chat | After collecting everyone’s availability, the Moderator follows up through Taeho’s Personal Agent. **“5:30 doesn’t work for everyone. Could you make it to the main gate by 5?”** **Taeho** “Yeah, I can leave a little earlier. Five works.” |
| 15 | Moderator · Final confirmation card in group chat | **“Tuesday, 5:00–5:30 PM · Meet at the main gate to review the cafeteria comparison mockup.”** All three select **Confirm** on their own screens. The group sees a single status, **“Everyone confirmed.”** Individual replies and the order of confirmation stay private. End the demo here. |
